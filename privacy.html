Privacy policy

MusicMinchas · last updated 12 October 2026

MusicMinchas is a personal, non-commercial music player for Android. This policy explains what data it uses and where that data goes. In short: your data stays on your phone, and the app has no servers of its own.

Google user data

If you connect Google Drive, MusicMinchas asks for read-only access to your Drive (drive.readonly) and your email address. It uses this access only to list the folders you choose, read the audio files in them (including their tags and cover art) and play or download them on your phone. It never changes, deletes or shares your Drive files.

The access token is kept by Google Play services on your phone. File lists and song details are stored only in the app's database on your phone. No Google user data is sent to the developer or to any third party, used for advertising, or sold.

MusicMinchas's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect Google Drive in the app's settings at any time, and revoke the app's access in your Google Account permissions.

Data kept on your phone

Your library, play history, favourites, playlists, downloaded songs and settings are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them (downloaded songs stay in the phone's Music folder until you delete them).

Online services the app uses

To complete song details and artwork, MusicMinchas sends song, artist and album names to:

  • MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive (song details, genres, cover art)
  • Deezer and Wikidata (artist photos)
  • LRCLIB (lyrics)

These requests contain no personal information about you. Looking up missing song details online can be turned off in the settings.

Last.fm (optional)

If you connect a Last.fm account, the songs you play (artist, title, album, time played) and the songs you mark as favourites are sent to Last.fm, and your scrobble history and loved tracks are read from it. This happens only while an account is connected; you can disconnect it in the settings. Last.fm's own privacy policy applies to the data held there.

Sonos speakers (optional)

When you play on a Sonos speaker, the speaker fetches the songs from the app over your home network. Nothing leaves your network for this.

No tracking

MusicMinchas contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
