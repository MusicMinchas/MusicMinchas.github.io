MusicMinchas

A personal music player for Android.

MusicMinchas plays your own music collection: songs in folders on your phone and in folders you choose in your Google Drive, streamed without downloading them first, or downloaded to the phone when you ask. It reads song details from the files and fills gaps from MusicBrainz, follows your favourite artists' releases, concerts and news, shows lyrics, keeps favourites and playlists, can play on Sonos speakers, and can scrobble to Last.fm.

It is a non-commercial app made for personal use. Your library, play history and settings stay on your phone.
